## Overview

Effective teamwork and communication are critical parts of the design of safe systems. In this course, you’ll learn what makes an effective team through case studies from health care and elsewhere. You’ll analyze the effects of individual behavior for promoting teamwork, communication, and a culture of safety. You will learn several essential communication tools, and you will learn how to prevent common problems associated with lapses in communication during inherently risky health care situations.

## Course Objectives

After completing this course, you will be able to:

- Explain how individual behavior and team dynamics in health care can make care safer or less safe.
- Use structured communication techniques to improve communication within health care.
- Specify possible interventions to improve patient safety and reduce risk during times of transition.

## Lessons

- **Lesson 1: Fundamentals of Teamwork and Communication**
This lesson discusses why effective team functioning is so critical to protecting patients from harm, including common types of errors associated with ineffective teamwork and communication. You’ll learn to distinguish teams that are working effectively from those that are not.
 
- **Lesson 2: Tools and Techniques for Effective Communication**
This lesson provides specific techniques teams can use to improve their communication, such as SBAR (Situation-Background-Assessment-Recommendation), critical language, and briefings.
 
- **Lesson 3: Safety During Transitions Across the Continuum of Care Course**
This lesson reviews some of the most common problems associated with miscommunication during critical transitions in health care — when responsibility for a patient is transferred between providers and/or locations — and how to prevent them.
 
**Estimated Time of Completion**: 1 hour 15 minutes
